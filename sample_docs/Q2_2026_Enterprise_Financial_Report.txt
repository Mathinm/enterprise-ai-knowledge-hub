========================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
========================================================

TechCorp Enterprises delivered strong second-quarter results, with total revenue of $142.8 million, representing a 23.4% increase year-over-year (Q2 FY2025: $115.7 million). Gross profit improved to $84.6 million (59.3% margin), up from $64.2 million (55.5% margin) in Q2 FY2025.

Net income for Q2 FY2026 was $18.4 million, compared to $9.8 million in Q2 FY2025 — an 87.8% increase. Adjusted EBITDA reached $31.2 million (21.8% margin). The company maintains a strong cash position of $267.4 million as of June 30, 2026.

# HR Policy Handbook 2026
## TechCorp Enterprises — Human Resources Division

---

## 1. Introduction & Scope

This handbook outlines the official Human Resources policies and procedures for all TechCorp Enterprises employees globally. These policies are effective from January 1, 2026, and supersede all prior versions.

All employees, contractors, and interns operating under TechCorp Enterprises are required to read, understand, and comply with the policies described in this handbook.

---

## 2. Employment Classifications

### 2.1 Full-Time Employees
Employees scheduled to work 40 hours or more per week. Full-time employees are eligible for all benefits described in Section 5.

### 2.2 Part-Time Employees
Employees working fewer than 32 hours per week. Part-time employees receive prorated benefits.

### 2.3 Contractors & Consultants
Non-employee personnel engaged for specific projects. Contractors are not eligible for company benefits but are subject to the Code of Conduct and Confidentiality Agreement.

---

## 3. Work Arrangement Policy

### 3.1 Remote Work
TechCorp supports a hybrid-first work culture. Employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week** by default. A fully remote arrangement (5 days/week) requires written approval from the department VP and HR Director.

Remote employees must:
- Maintain a reliable internet connection of at least 25 Mbps.
- Be available during core hours: **10:00 AM – 4:00 PM in their local time zone**.
- Attend all team stand-ups and critical meetings via video conference.
- Ensure their remote workspace meets safety and ergonomic standards as outlined in Appendix B.

### 3.2 Flexible Hours
Employees may flex their working hours outside core hours with manager approval, provided they complete 40 hours per week and attend scheduled team meetings.

### 3.3 International Remote Work
Working remotely from a country different from your employment country requires prior HR and Legal approval and may have tax and compliance implications. Such requests must be submitted at least 30 days in advance.

## 4. Leave Policies

### 4.1 Annual Leave (PTO)
- **Years 0–2**: 15 days per year
- **Years 3–5**: 18 days per year
- **Years 6+**: 22 days per year

PTO accrues monthly and can be carried over up to **10 days** into the next calendar year. Any excess is forfeited unless an exception is granted.

### 4.2 Sick Leave
Employees receive **10 sick days per year**. Sick leave does not carry over. A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.

### 4.3 Parental Leave

#### Birthing Parent
- **16 weeks** fully paid leave commencing from the birth/adoption date.
- Optional additional 8 weeks unpaid leave.

#### Non-Birthing Parent / Secondary Caregiver
- **6 weeks** fully paid leave within the first 12 months of birth/adoption.

#### Adoption Leave
Equivalent to birthing parent leave regardless of the employee's role.

All parental leave requests must be submitted **at least 8 weeks** in advance unless medical circumstances prevent this.

### 4.4 Bereavement Leave
- **5 days** for immediate family (spouse, parent, child, sibling).
- **3 days** for extended family (grandparent, aunt, uncle, in-laws).
- Additional unpaid days may be granted at manager's discretion.

### 4.5 Jury Duty & Civic Leave
Employees summoned for jury duty or civic service will receive full pay for up to **15 working days** per year.

## 5. Compensation & Benefits

### 5.1 Salary Review Cycle
Annual merit-based salary reviews occur in **March** each year. Performance ratings determine the salary increase band:
- **Exceeds Expectations (EE)**: 8–12% increase
- **Meets Expectations (ME)**: 3–7% increase
- **Needs Improvement (NI)**: 0% increase + Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

### 5.2 Health Insurance
TechCorp covers **80% of the premium** for employee health insurance and **60%** for dependents under its preferred provider network. Dental and vision plans are available as optional add-ons.

### 5.3 Retirement Plan (401k)
TechCorp matches employee 401k contributions up to **5% of base salary**. Vesting schedule:
- 25% after Year 1
- 50% after Year 2
- 75% after Year 3
- 100% after Year 4

### 5.4 Professional Development
Each full-time employee receives an annual **$2,500 Learning & Development budget** for:
- Online courses (Coursera, Udemy, LinkedIn Learning)
- Conferences and seminars
- Certification exams and study materials
- Books and subscriptions

### 5.5 Wellness Benefit
A **$500 annual wellness stipend** is available for gym memberships, fitness equipment, mental health apps, or wellness activities.

## 6. Code of Conduct

### 6.1 Respect & Inclusion
TechCorp is committed to maintaining a workplace that is free of discrimination, harassment, and bullying. All employees must treat colleagues, clients, and partners with professionalism and respect regardless of race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or national origin.

### 6.2 Confidentiality
All employees must safeguard company confidential information including trade secrets, client data, financial projections, and intellectual property. Violations may result in immediate termination and legal action.

### 6.3 Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any potential conflicts of interest to HR immediately. This includes outside employment, financial interests in competitors, or personal relationships with vendors.

### 6.4 Acceptable Use of Technology
Company IT systems are for business use. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with productivity or security. Employees must not use company systems to access illegal content, install unauthorized software, or circumvent security controls.

---

## 7. Performance Management

### 7.1 Performance Review Cycle
- **Mid-Year Check-In**: June – informal progress review with manager.
- **Annual Review**: December – formal performance evaluation with rating and feedback.

### 7.2 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
Employees rated "Needs Improvement" will be placed on a 90-day PIP with specific, measurable goals. Failure to meet PIP goals may result in role transition or termination.

---

## 8. Grievance & Escalation

Employees who believe a policy has been violated or who experience workplace issues should:
1. Attempt resolution directly with the involved party.
2. Escalate to their direct manager if unresolved.
3. Contact the HR Business Partner for their business unit.
4. Submit a formal grievance via the HR portal (hr.techcorp.com/grievance).

All complaints are treated confidentially to the extent legally permissible.

---

## 9. Policy Updates

TechCorp reserves the right to update these policies at any time. Employees will be notified of significant changes via company email and the HR portal. It is the employee's responsibility to review and comply with the most current version.

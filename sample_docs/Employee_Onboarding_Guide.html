Welcome to TechCorp Enterprises – New Employee Onboarding Guide

Congratulations on joining TechCorp Enterprises! This guide will walk you through everything you need to know during your first 90 days, from IT setup to understanding our culture, tools, and the expectations that will set you up for success.

Before Day 1: Pre-Onboarding Checklist

  • Sign and return your employment contract via DocuSign (check your personal email).
  • Complete your I-9/background verification through our partner, HireRight.
  • Set up your TechCorp email (format: firstname.lastname@techcorp.com). Instructions were emailed to your personal account.
  • Download Microsoft Teams, Slack, and the TechCorp VPN client from the IT Self-Service Portal: it.techcorp.com.
  • Review your benefits enrollment package. You have 30 days from your start date to enroll.

Day 1: Your First Day

Morning – Welcome Session (9:00 AM – 12:00 PM)

All new hires attend a virtual or in-person Welcome Session run by the HR Onboarding team. This session covers:

  • Company mission, values, and strategic priorities for 2026.
  • Organizational structure overview (meet your HR Business Partner).
  • Introduction to TechCorp's core tools: Microsoft 365, Jira, Confluence, GitHub, Slack.
  • Office tour (in-person) or virtual team introductions (remote).

Afternoon – IT Setup (1:00 PM – 4:00 PM)

  • Collect your company hardware: MacBook Pro 14" (engineers) or MacBook Pro 16" (ML engineers), iPhone 15 (if applicable).
  • Set up company email, Slack, Teams, and GitHub access with your IT Buddy.
  • Enable MFA on your TechCorp account (required before accessing any systems).
  • Install required software: VS Code / JetBrains IDE, Docker Desktop, TechCorp VPN.
  • Submit your SSO access requests via the IT Portal for your team's specific tools.

30-60-90 Day Plan

Days 1–30: Learn & Observe

Your primary goal in the first month is to absorb as much context as possible. Success metrics:

  • Complete all mandatory training modules (Security, Code of Conduct, GDPR awareness).
  • Shadow 3–5 team members across different sub-disciplines.
  • Read and understand the team's OKRs for H2 2026.
  • Set up your local development environment and successfully build and run the main project locally.
  • Attend at least 2 customer-facing calls (observer mode).
  • Complete your 30-day review with your manager by Day 30.

Days 31–60: Contribute & Build

Begin making tangible contributions and expanding your network within TechCorp:

  • Independently resolve at least 3 Jira tickets or contribute to 2 project features.
  • Lead at least 1 team meeting or present in a team stand-up.
  • Identify one process or documentation gap and propose an improvement.
  • Schedule coffee chats with 5 colleagues outside your immediate team (use the TechConnect platform).
  • Enroll in at least 1 course from your L&D budget ($2,500/year available from Day 1).
  • Complete your 60-day review with your manager by Day 60.

Days 61–90: Lead & Deliver

By the end of 90 days, you should be operating independently and delivering measurable outcomes:

  • Own a significant deliverable end-to-end (feature, analysis, client presentation, etc.).
  • Mentor or assist a fellow new hire who joined after you.
  • Co-create your personal OKRs for Q4 2026 with your manager.
  • Present your 90-day learnings and an improvement suggestion to your team or manager.
  • Complete your official 90-day performance check-in.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do I get paid?
TechCorp runs bi-weekly payroll every other Friday. Your first paycheck may be delayed by up to 2 weeks depending on your start date. Check Workday for your payslip details.
How do I request time off?
Submit all leave requests through Workday. Requests exceeding 5 consecutive days require manager approval at least 2 weeks in advance.
Can I use my L&D budget immediately?
Yes! Your $2,500 annual L&D budget is available from Day 1. Submit reimbursement requests through Workday with a receipt and business justification.
What if I need additional software or hardware?
Submit a request via the IT Self-Service Portal at it.techcorp.com. Standard approvals take 3–5 business days. Executive approval is required for requests over $500.
Where can I find company policies?
All policies are available on the HR Portal at hr.techcorp.com/policies. The HR Policy Handbook is the authoritative source.
